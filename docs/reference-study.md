The four unique reference Shorts were downloaded to temporary local storage for analysis. Contact sheets were inspected, and the signed-in Antigravity CLI analyzed MP3 audio through its native `read_file` media tool. A follow-up audit verified a successful audio-file read. These are AI-assisted observations, not fingerprinted song identifications. Source footage and audio are not distributed in this repository.

## Applied in Clipmax 0.9

- Short phrases with a bold outline, word highlighting and a small pop/slide entrance; steady and clean alternatives remain available.
- Original acoustic, fiddle/pluck and orchestral-style backgrounds alongside lo-fi, comic, cinematic and suspense arrangements.
- Tight bass drops, rimshots, rewind accents, camera snaps, sparkle reveals, error bloops, cartoon slides, energy hits and glass accents.
- Context-sensitive accent placement, varied music families and a persistent per-clip arrangement seed. A new variation can be previewed before saving.
- Original music/speech can be preserved with background/effects off. Custom sound uploads remain available for specific samples you can publish.

The built-in sounds are original synthesized designs in these broad categories. They are not the referenced creators' tracks, voices or exact recordings.
