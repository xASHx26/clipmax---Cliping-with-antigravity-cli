Accounts & quota
Provider-reported usage for Antigravity accounts seen by Clipmax.
Antigravity CLI
Manage sign-in and open the official quota panel in Antigravity. Run /usage there, then return here to see the reported balance.
Automatic account handoff is unavailable with this CLI connection. Each card is a saved usage report, not a separately connected credential. Clipmax pauses new Gemini work at 15% reported remaining quota; after a reset or official account change, refresh usage and resume from saved captions.
Quota percentages and reset times come from Antigravity. Conversation token counts describe one AI session and do not represent the account’s remaining balance.

Connect YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, Threads and Bluesky through Upload-Post. It manages social authorization and receives the videos you publish through it.
An Upload-Post account and one API key are required. Its free plan currently excludes TikTok; TikTok requires a paid plan. 
Create a publishing profile
Open account sign-in below, authorize your channels, then return and refresh. Clipmax verifies the account identities before enabling automatic uploads.
Sign in using your normal browser. When publishing, Clipmax opens the upload page, copies the caption and shows your video file. Finish the upload and post in the browser. No platform app details required.
One-time platform app setup
Direct API connections use your own registered developer app. There is no Clipmax publishing subscription. Platform quotas, review and approval still apply.
YouTube: enable YouTube Data API in Google Cloud, configure the consent screen and create a Desktop OAuth client. TikTok: register a Desktop app with Login Kit and approved video.publish. Unaudited TikTok integrations may be limited to private uploads.
Your password is entered only on the platform’s official website. App credentials and tokens are saved locally with Windows encryption in the desktop app.
